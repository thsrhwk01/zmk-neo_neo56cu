dfu-util 0.11
=============

The Windows flasher bundle includes dfu-util.exe and libusb-1.0.dll from the
official dfu-util 0.11 binary distribution:

https://dfu-util.sourceforge.net/
https://sourceforge.net/projects/dfu-util/files/

dfu-util is distributed under the GNU General Public License version 2 or
later. The bundle's third-party/dfu-util directory contains the upstream
COPYING file, binary distribution notes, and the complete corresponding
dfu-util-0.11.tar.gz source archive.

The included libusb-1.0.dll was built upstream from libusb commit
1a906274a66dd58bf81836db1306902d4a7dc185. Its corresponding source archive is
also included in third-party/dfu-util. See the license files within that source
archive for libusb's licensing terms.
